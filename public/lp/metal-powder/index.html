05 / Before you order

A few things worth agreeing upfront.

Especially for qualification, repeat orders and critical applications.

How do I keep repeat orders consistent?

Share your approved specification and previous batch or order reference. Ask for confirmation of the proposed source, batch evidence and any differences. Source-change approval and sample-to-bulk requirements should be agreed in writing before purchase.

What if I am not sure which powder I need?

Select “Not sure” and describe the application, process or equipment. Include any existing material specification. The enquiry can begin with incomplete information; final suitability and acceptance criteria still need to be confirmed.

What if I need special tests or have a quality concern?

List the required tests, methods and acceptance criteria before ordering. For a concern after receipt, keep the lot label and packaging information, and share the relevant report or test result. Testing scope, review responsibilities and any remedy must follow the agreed order terms.
