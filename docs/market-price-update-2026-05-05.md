# Market price update - 2026-05-05

Purpose: refresh the public-facing pricing system without turning the site into a live quote sheet. The product cards remain "From USD/kg" budget starting points, while the price guide explains realistic bands and RFQ variables.

## Pricing stance

- Use AM-grade gas atomized powder as the comparison basis.
- Prefer practical export/bulk-starting prices over small retail pack prices.
- Do not copy the lowest marketplace quote when the source does not show PSD, atomization route, packaging, or certificate scope.
- Keep all public prices explicitly non-binding; formal quotes still depend on lot size, PSD, documents, packaging, destination, and commercial terms.
