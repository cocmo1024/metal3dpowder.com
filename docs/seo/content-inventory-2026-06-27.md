## Site positioning

Metal3DPowder.com is a B2B metal powder knowledge site for overseas buyers, AM engineers, application owners, and sourcing teams evaluating powder by alloy, process route, and part family. The business goal is to attract powder inquiries from application domains where material choice, process qualification, service environment, and documentation affect buying decisions.

The site should stay close to:

- alloy and powder name
- LPBF, DED, MIM, binder jet, EBM, PSD, oxygen, flowability, and traceability
- application family and part class
- RFQ information that helps the buyer describe the powder or printed part correctly

## Content quality guardrails

The article intentionally avoids claiming that printed 316L is qualified for reactor pressure boundaries, core hardware, fuel hardware, or safety-related service. It frames nuclear work around classification, code path, owner requirements, traceability, inspection, leak testing, and qualification evidence.

The page is an application-led powder article, not a general SMR news page. The recent AI/electricity/SMR context is used only as a market signal. The durable SEO value is in alloy, powder, process, part family, and RFQ requirements.
