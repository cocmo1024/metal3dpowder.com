# Content Inventory - 2026-06-17

## Site position

Metal3dPowder.com is a B2B metal powder knowledge and product site for overseas buyers, engineers, sourcing teams, and AM/MIM/binder jet decision makers. The content should stay close to alloy, powder quality, process route, and application fit. It should not drift into generic robotics, generic automation, procurement advice, or unsupported supplier claims.

## Long-term content direction

The next content waves should keep following one-material, one-process, one-application intent rather than broad industry commentary. Good future lanes are:

- tool-side robotics and automation hardware where material choice changes wear, precision, cycle life, or wrist mass
- semiconductor and photonics fluid/thermal hardware where powder cleanliness, internal channels, and inspection matter
- power electronics and AI infrastructure cooling parts where copper/stainless choices map to real RF, thermal, and coolant requirements
- aerospace, defense sustainment, and hot-gas hardware where the alloy choice is tied to a specific load, temperature, or corrosion problem
- MIM and binder jet stainless production pages where scale, sintering, and powder economics define the buyer question
