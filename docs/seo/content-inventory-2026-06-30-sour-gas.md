Quality guardrails used in the article:

- Do not claim printed IN625 is automatically acceptable for sour service.
- Do not present IN625 as a universal oil and gas alloy.
- Separate corrosion-critical sour service from stronger stainless oil and gas hardware.
- State that pressure-containing, pressure-adjacent, wetted insert, and non-pressure support parts require different review levels.
- Keep the page powder-led through PSD, chemistry, oxygen, morphology, traceability, post-processing, inspection, and qualification data.
