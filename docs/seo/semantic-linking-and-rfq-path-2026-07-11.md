# Semantic Linking And RFQ Path - 2026-07-11

## Goal

Improve the long-term path from organic discovery to a qualified powder inquiry without adding overlapping content or changing established URLs.

## Inventory Result

- 114 Markdown articles were checked against the material, application, and process hub definitions.
- All 114 articles belong to at least one content hub.
- The existing metal powder specification guide already covers PSD, chemistry, oxygen, flowability, COA, and RFQ inputs, so a second RFQ guide would duplicate intent.

## Changes Applied

- Replaced popularity-first related article ranking with semantic scoring.
- Related pages now combine shared material and application hubs with process hubs, exact tags, and category, with enough application weight to distinguish specific end-use intent inside one alloy family.
- Removed hard-coded page-view values from the related-content decision path.
- Tightened the Semiconductor Fluid Hardware hub from nine mixed-intent pages to three pages with explicit semiconductor equipment intent.
- Added one reusable RFQ email builder with a prefilled subject and buyer checklist.
- Connected the prefilled RFQ email action to article CTAs, content hub CTAs, the contact page, and the existing homepage contact area.
- Retained the contact page and copy-email alternatives for buyers whose mail client is not configured.

## Expected Search And Inquiry Impact

- Tighter related links reinforce topical clusters and send crawlers and readers toward genuinely adjacent powder pages.
- Better article discovery should reduce generic recommendations that share only broad AM terminology.
- Prefilled RFQ emails reduce friction while asking for the fields needed for a useful technical and commercial response.
- No new indexable URL was created, so this update does not introduce cannibalization or thin content.
