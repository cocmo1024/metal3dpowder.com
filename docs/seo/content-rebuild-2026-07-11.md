## Google Standards Used

- Search Essentials: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials
- SEO Starter Guide: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide
- Helpful, reliable, people-first content: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content
- Spam policies and scaled content abuse: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies

The operating interpretation is:

- answer a real user task rather than producing keyword variants,
- add original decision value beyond a generic summary,
- use clear sources and bounded claims,
- keep the site's primary purpose focused,
- update or consolidate weak existing pages instead of publishing for freshness,
- avoid scaled pages whose main difference is an alloy/application phrase,
- judge success through Search Console and inquiries, not publishing volume.

## Image Decision

No new article images were added in this wave.

Industrial SEO pages do not become more helpful by repeating generic powder photos. Add an image only when it provides information that text or a table cannot provide efficiently, such as:

- a real PSD or morphology comparison,
- a labeled process or powder-flow diagram,
- a defect or surface-condition example,
- a part cutaway that explains trapped powder or cleaning access,
- a material/process decision chart that benefits from visual form.

Decorative AI images should not be a publishing requirement.
