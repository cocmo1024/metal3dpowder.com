The article intentionally avoids:

- claiming every fuel nozzle should be printed
- claiming the GE LEAP fuel nozzle is an IN718 part
- treating IN718 as a universal combustor material
- competing with Hastelloy X when oxidation or flame-adjacent hot-gas behavior dominates
- pretending LPBF removes the need for heat treatment, HIP, machining, inspection, cleaning, or flow verification

- IN718 as a strength-led precipitation-hardened nickel route

- LPBF powder and small-passage design constraints

- internal routing to IN718, Hastelloy X, IN625, energy, aerospace, nickel, and LPBF pages
