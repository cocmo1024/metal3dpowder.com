# Keyword Expansion Operating Plan - 2026-06-30

## Goal

Make metal3dpowder.com capable of ranking for many durable industrial powder queries while staying Google-friendly. The site should scale by building useful clusters, not by publishing low-information keyword variants.

## Positioning

The site is a B2B metal powder content engine for additive manufacturing and powder metallurgy buyers. Its strongest positioning is:

- metal powder material selection
- AM process fit
- application-specific powder use cases
- RFQ checklists for engineers and sourcing teams
- alloy tradeoffs by service environment

The content should lead users toward qualified powder inquiries from energy, aerospace, medical, semiconductor, tooling, robotics, marine, chemical processing, oil and gas, and thermal-management applications.

## Content Architecture

Every incremental topic should fit at least two existing structures:

- material hub, such as titanium, stainless, nickel, copper, tool steel, aluminum, or cobalt chrome powder
- application hub, such as energy, carbon capture, AI liquid cooling, aerospace, nuclear, marine, tooling, robotics, medical, oil and gas, or semiconductor hardware
- process hub, such as LPBF, DED, EBM, binder jet, or MIM
- comparison or specification page, when the search intent is a decision rather than a part family

Preferred topic formula:

`alloy + powder + specific application + process / environment / RFQ constraint`

Examples:

- IN625 powder for sour gas valves, chokes, and downhole flow hardware
- CuCrZr powder for co-packaged optics cold plates
- 316L powder for SMR auxiliary-fluid manifolds
- M300 powder for robot gripper jaws and end-of-arm tooling

## Publish Filters

Do not publish a new page unless it passes these filters:

- Powder intent is explicit.
- The application is specific enough to avoid generic industrial prose.
- Existing pages do not already satisfy the query.
- The page can link to at least one material hub, one application hub, and one process or specification page.
- The topic can include an RFQ checklist or decision logic.
- Recent context is used only when it strengthens evergreen search value.
- Claims are bounded; regulated, pressure, medical, aerospace, nuclear, and sour-service topics must avoid automatic approval language.

## Internal Link Standard

Each new article should include:

- 5 to 10 contextual links to older relevant articles
- 1 material hub link
- 1 application hub link
- 1 process hub or specification guide link
- 2 to 4 inbound links from older high-relevance pages

Each new hub should include:

- the new article
- the best existing material guide
- at least one comparison or specification page
- adjacent application pages that prevent dead ends

## Anti-Duplication Rules

Avoid:

- one page per synonym when the same service condition is already covered
- pages that discuss an industry but not powder selection
- broad market-news posts without a powder/material decision
- generic supplier, procurement, or sustainability pages unless tied to powder specs and RFQ behavior

Prefer:

- deeper pages for a real engineering decision
- cluster pages with clear hub routing
- application-specific RFQ content that helps a buyer ask for the right powder
- concise updates to existing pages when a new topic is only a sub-intent
