# Content Inventory - 2026-06-26

## Site positioning

Metal3DPowder.com is a B2B metal powder knowledge site for overseas buyers, AM engineers, application owners, and sourcing teams evaluating powder by alloy, process, and part family. The business objective is to attract real powder inquiries from application domains where material choice, process route, and service environment matter: energy, water, aerospace, medical, semiconductor, AI cooling, tooling, robotics, hydrogen, fusion, and industrial flow hardware.

The content should continue to avoid generic procurement posts. Each new page should connect alloy + powder + process + application + RFQ details so search traffic can become a qualified powder demand inquiry.

## Duplicate-prevention decision

The selected topic is not a duplicate of the seawater desalination page. Desalination content targets seawater, reverse-osmosis concentrate, pump/valve hardware, and water-treatment plant service. The new geothermal page targets hot brine, scaling, dissolved minerals, acid gases, heat-exchanger headers, valve inserts, repair overlays, and EGS/pilot-loop energy hardware.

The selected topic is also not a duplicate of the generic IN625 guide or the corrosion-heavy hot-side page. Those pages explain alloy-level or broad hot corrosive service. The new page narrows the search intent to geothermal brine flow hardware and powder RFQ requirements.

To capture more powder inquiries from application domains, continue expanding one application gap at a time instead of repeating alloy definitions:

- energy: geothermal brine, hydrogen, fusion, turbine, burner, hot-gas, high-heat-flux cooling
- water and industrial flow: desalination, chemical dosing, pump/valve hardware, clean manifolds
- data infrastructure: AI liquid cooling, co-packaged optics, power electronics, RF thermal hardware
- semiconductor and clean process: gas manifolds, sensor housings, chemical-side flow components
- robotics and industrial automation: lightweight joints, actuator housings, grippers, fixtures
- medical and dental: porous implant structures, dental frameworks, surgical and clean hardware
- aerospace and defense: sustainment spares, propulsion support, satellite and UAV hardware

Each page should provide a clear material decision, process route, RFQ checklist, and internal path back to material/process/application hubs.
