## Design Style

- Industrial, professional, restrained, and high-end.
- Typography should remain common, highly compatible sans-serif. Avoid uncommon decorative fonts.
- Font sizes should be controlled and professional. Avoid oversized blog titles, oversized section headings, and marketing-style hero typography.
- Layout should feel consistent across homepage, blog listings, tag pages, article pages, FAQ, Contact, Privacy, About, and Terms.
- Keep spacing compact but readable. Avoid crowded edges on mobile.
- Homepage should remain direct and commercial. The user explicitly prefers no unnecessary hero modules or marketing blocks.
- Blog and content pages should read like a clean industrial knowledge base, not a decorative magazine site.

## Important Notes

- The user wants SEO traffic, but content must stay accurate and professionally credible.
- The target visitor is an overseas buyer, engineer, sourcing person, or AM/MIM/binder jet decision maker looking for metal powder.
- Search intent should stay close to products and applications. Avoid drifting into broad supplier-management, logistics, procurement process, or generic manufacturing advice unless directly tied to powder selection.
- Before every content wave, audit existing articles first. If a topic already exists, improve the existing URL instead of creating a competing page.
- Preserve existing article URL structure under `/posts/Alloys/` because it is part of the SEO migration strategy.
- Keep sitemap quality high. Do not add low-value archives or tag pagination to the sitemap.
- Do not add sensitive keys, tokens, credentials, or private commercial terms to the repo.
- Build output goes to `dist`; do not manually edit generated files.

## Do Not Change Without Clear Reason

- Do not change homepage structure unless the user explicitly asks. Homepage should stay: powder products, contact path, footer.
- Do not replace the restrained industrial visual system with decorative, colorful, or marketing-heavy layouts.
- Do not rename existing article slugs or move article URLs without a redirect strategy.
- Do not remove existing legacy `/posts/Alloys/` URL compatibility.
- Do not add broad unrelated SEO content just to increase page count.
- Do not add visible company claims, certifications, or customer names unless they are verified by the user.
- Do not add fake testimonials, fake case studies, or unsupported performance claims.
- Do not change pricing casually. Price updates need conservative public references or user-provided data.
